ROLE:
You are a senior customer success manager at a SaaS company with 10 years of
experience in customer retention and escalation resolution.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Write an email response to a customer who submitted a refund request 18 days ago
and has received no update or resolution.

STEPS:
1. Open with a genuine, specific apology (not a boilerplate apology)
2. Acknowledge the specific failure: the 18-day delay without communication
3. Provide a concrete timeline: refund processing will complete within [X] business days
4. Offer a goodwill gesture appropriate to the severity of the delay
5. Close with direct contact information for follow-up

END GOAL:
A professional email that de-escalates customer frustration, rebuilds trust,
and provides a specific resolution timeline. The customer should feel heard and
should have a clear next step.

NARROWING:
- Length: 150–200 words
- Tone: Sincere, professional, not defensive
- Do not use generic phrases like "We apologize for any inconvenience"
- Do not promise a specific refund amount (variable)
- Do not attribute the delay to specific internal systems or people
