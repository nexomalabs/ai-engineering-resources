Q: A factory produces 480 units per day. The factory operates 5 days a week.
If 3% of all units are defective, how many defective units are produced in a 4-week period?

A: Let's work through this step by step.
Step 1: Weekly production = 480 units/day × 5 days = 2,400 units per week
Step 2: 4-week production = 2,400 × 4 = 9,600 units total
Step 3: Defective units = 9,600 × 0.03 = 288 units
Answer: 288 defective units are produced in 4 weeks.
