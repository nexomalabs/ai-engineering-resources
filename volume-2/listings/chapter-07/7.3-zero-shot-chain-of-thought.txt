Prompt:
If a train travels at 60 mph and needs to cover 225 miles, how many minutes
will the journey take? Let's think step by step.

Model Output:
Step 1: Calculate the time in hours.
Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 225 miles ÷ 60 mph = 3.75 hours

Step 2: Convert hours to minutes.
3.75 hours × 60 minutes/hour = 225 minutes

Answer: The journey will take 225 minutes.
